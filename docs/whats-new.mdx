## Configurator v3.19 (Oct 23 2026)

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="PenguPlay as a backup source" icon="sparkles">
    Fine-Tune → Extras offers PenguPlay, the HTTP stream addon Tam-Taro’s setups also carry. Paste the personal manifest URL from your pengu.uk account and it joins your build after every other stream source, serving streams only, so it fills gaps without leading. Without a URL that ends in /manifest.json nothing is added, since AIOStreams refuses the save otherwise. Hosts that block HTTP streams, ElfHosted among them, do not offer it. The URL stays on your device and in the config you install, never in share links.
