This page is regenerated from `configurator/src/data/changelog.js` by `scripts/sync-docs.py`, so it always reflects the latest shipped configurator version (currently **v3.54**).

## Configurator v3.54 (Nov 27 2026)

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Sharper Nuvio badges (AIO Enhanced)" icon="sparkles">
    a new switch in Fine-Tune → Stream cards adds invisible markers to your stream cards. The Core Neon · AIO Enhanced badge pack in Nuvio reads them, so badges show the resolution, HDR and audio AIOStreams parsed instead of guessing from the file name. Off unless you turn it on.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your card style stays the same" icon="bolt">
    the markers go in front of whichever style you picked and render as nothing. Checked with AIOStreams 2.38.1's own formatter engine on all 22 built-in styles (28 counting the Core score modes): the visible card is identical, and the 4K and Dolby Vision badges fire on a 4K DV stream and not on a 1080p SDR one.
  </Card>
  <Card title="The badge card offers the Enhanced pack when your setup carries the markers" icon="shield-check">
    after a Nuvio install, or in the manifest window's Nuvio tab. Re-importing a config with the markers turns the switch back on and recognises your style.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Only for Nuvio" icon="sliders">
    the markers are in the stream text for every app on that AIOStreams install. They are zero-width characters, but some TV apps draw them as small boxes, so leave the switch off if you also watch in Stremio on a TV.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Configurator v3.53 (Nov 27 2026)

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Stream badges after a Nuvio install" icon="sparkles">

  </Card>
  <Card title="No DV pack for screens without Dolby Vision" icon="bolt">

  </Card>
  <Card title="Customise your badges" icon="shield-check">
    a link from the same card opens the Core Badge Builder to choose your own badges, colours and order.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Generated output unchanged" icon="sliders">
    every reference config is byte-for-byte what 3.52 produced apart from the version stamp. Badges are imported into Nuvio from a link and stored there; they are not part of the AIOStreams config.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
