title: "Mobile Template"
description: "A 1080p template for phones, tablets and data-capped connections."
---

**Core Nexus Mobile** keeps streams small enough for mobile data and weaker Wi-Fi while staying at 1080p.

## What it does

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Resolution | 1080p first, then 720p and below |
| Bitrate ceiling | 25 Mbps |
| Size cap | 10 GB per movie, 3 GB per episode |
| Picture | SDR preferred: phones show HDR inconsistently, and SDR files are smaller |
| Excluded | AV1, 3D, and lossless audio (TrueHD, DTS-HD MA, DTS:X, FLAC) |

An hour of 1080p WEB-DL at typical bitrates uses roughly 2–4 GB, so a film under this template stays well inside a 10 GB cap.

## When to use it

- Watching on a phone or tablet, on mobile data or a capped plan.
- A home connection under about 30 Mbps, where 4K or REMUX files buffer.
- A second Stremio profile for travel, alongside a 4K template on the TV.

On a TV with a fast connection, a 1080p or 4K template from the [Template Directory](/template-directory) will look better.

## Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Import the template">
    Copy the **Mobile** import link from the [Template Directory](/template-directory#torbox-pro) and paste it into AIOStreams → **About → Get Started → Load Template**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your TorBox key">
    In AIOStreams → **Services**, paste your TorBox API key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and install">
    Click **Save**, copy the manifest URL and install it in Stremio or Nuvio on the device.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Want a different cap? In the [Configurator](/configurator), type your connection speed under **Connection speed · Mbps**. It limits bitrate to 80% of that speed, and keeps the setting between visits.
</Tip>
