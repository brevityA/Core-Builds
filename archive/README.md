# Archive

Point-in-time audits, plans and handoff notes. They describe the repository as it was on
their dates, and most of the defects they list have since been fixed. They are kept for
history only: nothing reads them, and none of them is current guidance.

Current sources of truth:

- `CLAUDE.md`: how the repo works and its known pitfalls
- `CHANGELOG.md` and `configurator/src/data/changelog.js`: what changed and when
- `ROADMAP.md`: what is planned (generated from the changelogs)
