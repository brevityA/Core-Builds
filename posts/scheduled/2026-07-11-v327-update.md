Hey Folks 👋

Labs got its biggest expression update yet — 7 new SEL features across all 6 nightly templates, plus a configurator fix that was breaking imports on self-hosted instances. Here's the rundown:

***

### 🧪 Labs v0.14.0 — 7 new SEL features

**1. Runtime-aware bitrate floors**

The REMUX bitrate floors were killing valid anime and animation encodes. Animation legitimately compresses ~50% smaller than live-action — a 4K anime REMUX at 25 Mbps is perfectly normal, but the 40 Mbps floor was treating it as fake. Same problem with short episodes (< 25 min).

Fixed: bitrate floors now check `isAnime`, `'Animation' in genres`, and `runtime < 25` before firing. If any match, the floor is bypassed entirely.

**2. Anime language passthrough**

`passthrough(streams, 'language')` — skips language filtering on anime queries. Fansub groups tag languages inconsistently (Japanese audio listed as English, dual-audio listed as mono, etc.) and the standard language filter was silently dropping valid streams. This was most visible on niche anime where every available stream had non-standard tagging.

**3. latestSeason-aware season pack kill**

Season packs for the *current* season are now preserved. Previously, if even one individual episode existed, the season pack got killed — even if the pack was the only high-quality source for a show that's still airing. Now only older-season packs are killed when episodes exist.

**4. Subtitle preference PSE**

Streams with embedded English subs get a scoring boost. Not a hard filter — streams without subs still appear. But when two streams are otherwise identical, the one with subs ranks higher. Subtle but useful for non-English audio content.

**5. Age sort key**

Newer uncached torrents rank above older ones. More recent uploads are more likely to have active seeders — straightforward tiebreaker for `uncachedMovies` and `uncachedSeries`.

**6–7. cachedAnime / uncachedAnime sort sections**

Dedicated sort sections for anime. SeaDex jumps to position 2 (right after cached status) — it's the strongest anime quality signal and was previously sitting at position 4. The uncached anime sort also promotes seeders and adds the age key.

***

### 🛠️ Configurator fix

The configurator was generating templates with 2 regex patterns (`Radarr HD Bluray T3 [B]` and `Anime LQ Groups`) that aren't in Vidhin05's 174-entry allowlist. Self-hosted AIOStreams instances were showing **"2/176 regexes not allowed"** on import. Both patterns removed — configurator now generates 101 scored patterns, all verified.

If you built a template with the configurator recently and got this error, just regenerate it.
