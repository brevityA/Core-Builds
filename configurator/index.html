Quick Start Guide

New to Core Builds? Here's how it works

Core Builds generates AIOStreams templates — config files that control how your streaming addon finds, filters, and sorts torrents and usenet results. Pick your service, device, and preferences, and we build the template for you.
1
Pick your debrid service
TorBox, AllDebrid, Real-Debrid, EasyNews, or go free with P2P/HTTP streaming. This determines which scrapers and presets your template uses.
Don't have a debrid account? Choose "Free Streaming" or "Free P2P" from the splash — no signup needed.
2
Choose your device
Fire Stick, Apple TV, Samsung TV, Android, PC, etc. This sets audio codec limits, HDR handling, and resolution caps automatically.
Not sure? "Generic" works for most devices — you can fine-tune later.
3
Set your resolution
4K or 1080p. This controls which quality tiers, regex scoring, and bitrate filters are applied to your streams.
4
Add your API key (optional)
Paste credentials only for the providers you select. Export JSON keeps them local; Direct Install sends the generated config to your chosen AIOStreams host.
5
Deploy
One click creates your config on the fastest AIOStreams instance and opens it in Stremio, WuPlay, or Nuvio. Or export the JSON to import manually.
Your template includes 14+ scrapers, smart sorting, regex scoring, and quality filters — all pre-configured based on your choices.

Welcome to Core Builds

Core Builds creates optimised streaming configs for Stremio and third-party applications that support Stremio addons. It sets up how your streams are found, filtered, sorted, and displayed — so you get the best results without manual tweaking.

🎬
What it does
Builds a template that tells AIOStreams how to find and rank your streams
🕒
How long
About a minute with Quick Install, or use Guided and Advanced routes for more control
🔒
Privacy
Credentials are not logged by Core Builds; Direct Install sends the config only to the host you choose
Takes about 30 seconds
